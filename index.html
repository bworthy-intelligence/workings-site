Workings is an AI-powered maths tutoring app that generates problems tailored to each learner, adapts to their level, and helps them build real understanding, one step at a time.

Built for Learners and Tutors

For Learners

🧠

Adaptive AI Problem Generation

Every practice session is different. Workings uses AI to generate fresh maths problems matched to the learner's current level, so they're always challenged: never bored, never overwhelmed. As they improve, the problems get harder. If they're struggling, the app eases back and builds confidence with simpler foundations first.

📐

Curriculum-Aligned Topics

Problems are organised by year level and strand — number, algebra, measurement, geometry and more — covering the key areas of the Australian maths curriculum from Year 1 through Year 10.

✏️

Show Your Workings

Students aren't just asked for a final answer. Workings encourages them to think through each step, building the habits that lead to real mathematical understanding rather than guessing.

📈

Progress Tracking

Sessions are saved so learners and their tutors can see exactly how they're progressing over time, which topics are strengths, and where more practice is needed.

🎯

Try Before You Sign Up

New to Workings? Try a free session without creating an account. When you're ready, a free account unlocks full progress tracking and an unlimited practice history.

For Tutors

📋

Instant Problem Generation

Generate a set of targeted problems for any topic and difficulty level in seconds. No more searching for worksheets or writing questions from scratch. Workings does it for you.

👩‍🏫

Session Planning

Plan and structure tutoring sessions around each learner's current needs. Choose topics, set difficulty, and let the app generate a fresh set of problems ready to go.

👥

Multiple Learner Profiles

Manage multiple learners under a single tutor account. Each learner gets their own profile, progress history, and adaptive difficulty calibrated to their individual level.

✅

Validated Problem Bank

AI-generated problems go through an independent verification pass before being stored. Tutors can also flag and review questions to ensure quality for their learners.
