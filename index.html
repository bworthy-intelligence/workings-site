Personalised maths practice that adapts to every learner's level. Tutors assign and track; parents see progress; students build real confidence session by session.

Built for Learners, Tutors and Parents

For Learners

🧠

Adaptive Practice

Every session is different. Problems are matched to the learner's current level so they're always challenged: never bored, never overwhelmed. As they improve, the difficulty increases. If they're struggling, the app eases back and rebuilds confidence from firmer ground.

📐

Curriculum-Aligned Topics

Problems are organised by year level and strand (number, algebra, measurement, geometry and more), covering the key areas of the Australian maths curriculum from Year 1 through Year 10.

✏️

Show Your Workings

Students aren't just asked for a final answer. When they're stuck, a built-in hint system nudges them through the reasoning, one step at a time, so they arrive at the answer through understanding, not guessing.

📈

Progress Tracking

Sessions are saved so learners and their tutors can see exactly how they're progressing over time, which topics are strengths, and where more practice is needed.

📚

Complete Tutor Homework

When a tutor assigns homework, it appears directly in your app. Work through each problem at your own pace, get hints when you're stuck, and track your progress as you go.

🎯

Try Before You Sign Up

New to Workings? Try a free session without creating an account. When you're ready, a free account unlocks full progress tracking and an unlimited practice history.

For Tutors

📋

Instant Problem Generation

Generate a set of targeted problems for any topic and difficulty level in seconds. No more searching for worksheets or writing questions from scratch. Workings does it for you.

📤

Assign Homework

Review a set of targeted problems, approve the ones that are right for your student, and send them directly to the learner's app. Students complete assignments in their own time and results are tracked automatically.

👥

Multiple Learner Profiles

Manage multiple learners under a single tutor account. Each learner gets their own profile, progress history, and adaptive difficulty calibrated to their individual level.

✅

Quality-Checked Problems

Every problem passes an independent quality check before entering the pool. Tutors then review and approve specific problems before they reach a student, so nothing gets sent without your sign-off.

For Parents

📈

See How They're Going

Track your child's session history, streaks and progress over time. See which topics they're working on and where they might need a little more support.

📋

Homework at a Glance

When a tutor assigns homework, you can see it too. Know what's been set, whether it's been completed, and how your child performed.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Multiple Children, One Account

Manage learner profiles for each of your children under a single account. Each child gets their own progress history and difficulty level calibrated to them individually.

🆓

Free to Start

Your child can try a free session without even creating an account. When you're ready, a free account unlocks full progress tracking and homework from tutors.
